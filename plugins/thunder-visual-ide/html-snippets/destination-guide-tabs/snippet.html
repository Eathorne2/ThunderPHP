Local table

Book one dinner, leave the rest open.

Mix traditional tascas, contemporary Portuguese cooking and market lunches. Ask hotel staff for neighbourhood suggestions rather than only landmark addresses.

Practical

Walk, tram, ferry and metro.

The centre is compact but steep. Comfortable shoes matter, and a reusable transport card simplifies metro, tram and ferry journeys.

Weather

Spring and early autumn feel effortless.

April–June and September–October balance warm days with manageable crowds. Summer is lively and hot; winter is mild and quieter.
