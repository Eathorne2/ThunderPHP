How to design a daily workflow people will actually use

The best operating systems reduce uncertainty without turning every task into administrative work.

Start with the decisions

Before adding fields, statuses, and dashboards, list the decisions a person must make during a normal day. The interface should make those decisions easier.

Keep progress visible

People should be able to see what is ready, what is blocked, and what needs attention without opening every record individually.

Good workflow software gives the team confidence about what happens next.

Measure the useful outcomes

Track completion, turnaround time, and unresolved work. Avoid metrics that look impressive but do not improve the next decision.
