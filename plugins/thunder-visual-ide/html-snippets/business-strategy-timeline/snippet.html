Engagement model

From ambiguity to accountable action.

  1. 01
    Frame

    Align leaders on the question, scope and decision criteria.

  2. 02
    Diagnose

    Combine evidence, interviews and market context into a clear point of view.

  3. 03
    Design

    Build practical options and test the operational implications.

  4. 04
    Deliver

    Translate the choice into milestones, owners and measurable outcomes.
