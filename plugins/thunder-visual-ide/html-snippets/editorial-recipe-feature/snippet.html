Recipe 024 Weeknight comfort
From the autumn issue

Crisp-edged dumplings with ginger broth.

A generous one-pan supper with a deeply savoury broth, soft greens and dumplings that steam and crisp at the same time.

Prep
25 min
Cook
30 min
Level
Easy
Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 16 frozen dumplings
  • 2 cups vegetable stock
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 handfuls leafy greens

Method

  1. 01

    Warm a wide pan with a little oil. Arrange the dumplings flat-side down and cook until golden.

  2. 02

    Add stock, soy and ginger. Cover immediately and steam for eight minutes.

  3. 03

    Remove the lid, add the greens and simmer until the broth reduces slightly.

  4. 04

    Finish with sesame oil, spring onion and chilli crisp before serving.
